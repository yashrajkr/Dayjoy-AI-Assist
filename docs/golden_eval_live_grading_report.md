### I'm suffering from seasonal allergies, what should I take?
*category=recommendation source=web_search addresses_question=None*

For seasonal allergies most people find relief with a combination of an oral antihistamine and a steroid nasal spray:

* **Oral antihistamines** – second‑generation options such as cetirizine (Zyrtec), loratadine (Claritin) or fexofenadine (Allegra) work well for sneezing, runny nose and itchy eyes and usually cause little drowsiness【https://www.southshorehealth.org/wellness/blog/six-ways-alleviate-seasonal-allergies】.  

* **Steroid nasal sprays** – fluticasone (Flonase), triamcinolone (Nasacort) or budesonide (Rhinocort) are the first‑line treatment for nasal congestion and inflammation. They may take a few days to a week to reach full effect, so start them a couple of weeks before the pollen season and use them consistently【https://www.cbwchc.org/news/guide-to-managing-seasonal-allergies】.

If you need quick relief from nasal stuffiness, a short‑acting oral decongestant (e.g., pseudoephedrine/Sudafed) can be added, but it should not be used for more than a few days.

**Important:** These are general suggestions. Your exact needs can vary based on other health conditions, medications you’re taking, and how severe your symptoms are. Please talk to your primary‑care provider or an allergist before starting any new medication.

### How do I return a damaged product?
*category=policy source=web_search addresses_question=None*

Sure! Here’s a quick guide to returning a damaged product:

1. **Check the seller’s return policy** – Look for any specific instructions about damaged items (e.g., time limits, required documentation).

2. **Gather evidence** – Take clear photos or a short video showing the damage. This helps speed up the approval process.

3. **Contact customer support** – Reach out via the seller’s website, app, or email. Mention that the item arrived damaged, attach the photos, and request a return or replacement.

4. **Get a return authorization** – The seller will usually issue a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and provide a prepaid shipping label.

5. **Pack the item securely** – Use the original packaging if possible, include all accessories, and attach the return label. If the original box is damaged, use a sturdy box and add cushioning.

6. **Ship it back** – Drop the package at the designated carrier (often UPS, FedEx, DHL, etc.) and keep the receipt or tracking number.

7. **Follow up** – Once the seller receives the return, they’ll process a refund or ship a replacement, depending on what you requested.

If you don’t see a clear return option on the order page, look for a “Help” or “Contact Us” link, or check the seller’s FAQ for damaged‑goods procedures.
